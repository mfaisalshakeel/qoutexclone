# Progress log

Newest first. One entry per finished roadmap task: date, task, what changed, how it was verified.

## Log

- **2026-09-17**: Phase 1 — **OTC price engine**. `engine/otc.ts` replaces the single random walk with a per-market broker price process: seeded mulberry32 RNG carried inside the state, GARCH(1,1) volatility clustering (kept stationary by construction), alternating trend and range regimes of random length, drift while trending, mean reversion to a slowly drifting anchor, bounded spikes, and a hard per-tick cap that holds *after* rounding so the tape can never gap. The module imports nothing at all — a test asserts that structurally, and a second test smuggles position/exposure/user fields into the tick input and proves the path is byte-identical. An integration test goes further and drives the real feed against a database flooded with 50 one-sided open positions (then flipped to the other side), asserting identical printed paths. State persists to `OtcMarketState` every 15s and on shutdown, and is resumed at boot: verified live across a restart, where the price continued within 0.07% of the persisted value instead of jumping back to the seed. Markets tick on their own configurable interval, so the feed now measures real elapsed time (with an injectable clock — which also revealed that two feed tests had quietly become vacuous, now fixed). Each market carries per-asset overrides in `Asset.otcConfig`, editable from a new admin **Price engine** screen with a server-rendered candle preview before saving; changes apply to the running feed with no restart. OTC markets can optionally ease their anchor toward the matching exchange market's price while that market is open. 14 unit tests, 1 integration test, 2 e2e specs.

- **2026-09-17**: Phase 1 — **Trading sessions**. New `TradingSchedule` / `ScheduleWindow` / `MarketHoliday` models with `Asset.scheduleId` (null = always open, which is how OTC and crypto trade). `lib/sessions.ts` holds the pure weekly-hours maths — windows normalised and merged into week ranges, sessions that run past midnight or wrap Saturday into Sunday, holidays as whole UTC days, next-open/next-close resolution — with 14 unit tests. `services/market-hours.ts` caches the calendars and reloads them when an operator edits one. Five seeded calendars (forex 24/5, metals and energy, US equities, US index CFDs, European index CFDs) cover 40 exchange-traded markets. `placeTrade` now rejects a closed market with `market_closed`, the next open time and its OTC twin; the catalogue endpoint reports `isOpen`, `nextOpen`, `nextClose`, `holiday`, `schedule` and `otcAlternative`; the terminal dims and badges closed markets, and the ticket becomes a panel explaining when it reopens with a button that switches to the OTC market. A closed exchange also stops printing prices (the feed skips it and the last tick stands) — verified live: 10 closed markets frozen over 6 seconds while all 79 open ones kept moving. Admin gains a Sessions screen (weekly hours per calendar, edit a window's hours, add and remove holidays, live open/closed state) plus market-class filtering and session columns on the Markets screen. 2 e2e specs, one of which closes forex with a real holiday and checks the API, the server guard and the UI. Also removed the terminal's duplicate ticket mount: the layout now renders one ticket and one positions panel chosen by a `useMediaQuery` hook instead of mounting both and hiding one, which halves that work and removes the ambiguity that kept breaking selectors.

- **2026-09-17**: Phase 1 — **Market catalogue seed**. `server/src/data/markets.ts` holds 89 markets: 25 currency pairs (majors, crosses, exotics) each with an OTC twin, 12 crypto vs USDT (BTC and ETH also OTC), gold/silver/WTI (all with OTC), 10 large-cap stocks (NVDA and TSLA OTC) and 5 indices (US500 and US100 OTC). Per-market base price, per-minute volatility, precision, pip size, payout and icon; OTC twins carry a +3% payout premium and slightly higher volatility, and never point at an exchange symbol. A second hand-written migration moved the original crypto markets to the exchange convention (BTC/USD → BTC/USDT) and renamed the denormalised `Trade.symbol` in step, so existing history stays readable. The market list now leads with the pair, badges OTC and shows the payout. Verified: 7 catalogue unit tests, 89 markets priced through the API, search across classes, a live GBP/USD trade placed and settled at 5-decimal precision, no console errors at 1440px or 390px, 13 e2e green. Tick buffers were re-sized (8000 → 600 per market) now that 89 markets tick at once; settlement only ever needs the tick at expiry.

## Decisions

_Record product choices made without the owner here: what, why, where it's configurable._

- **E2E browser binary**: `playwright.config.ts` honours `E2E_CHROMIUM_PATH` so sandboxes with a pre-installed Chromium don't download another. CI installs the pinned browser and ignores the variable.
- **Prettier scope**: code only. `*.md` is ignored so the owner's docs keep their own formatting.
- **Settings vs env**: env stays the bootstrap (DSN, secrets, ports, feed provider) and seeds the *defaults* of runtime keys; anything an operator would change while running lives in the settings registry. Existing deployments keep their current behaviour until someone edits a value in admin.
- **One engine for every simulated market**: rather than keep the old random walk for exchange-traded markets and add a second engine for OTC, all simulated pricing runs through the OTC engine. Non-OTC markets keep their session gating and will be driven by a real provider in the "Real feeds" task; OTC markets are broker-priced by definition.
- **RNG state column**: mulberry32's state is a uint32, which does not fit MySQL's signed `INT`. The column is `INT UNSIGNED` (found by running it — the first flush failed with "out of range").
- **Session ownership**: calendars are shared records (forex, metals/energy, US equities, US/European index CFDs) rather than per-asset hours, so one edit moves every market on that venue. Crypto and OTC deliberately carry no calendar.
- **Closed markets freeze**: rather than keep generating candles nobody can trade, the feed skips markets outside their session. The last tick stands until the market reopens.
- **Crypto quote currency**: the original markets were BTC/USD; the catalogue quotes crypto in USDT because that is what the exchange feed prices. A data migration renamed the assets and their denormalised trade symbols rather than leaving two conventions in the table.
- **OTC pricing premium**: OTC twins get +3% payout (capped at 95%) and 1.15× volatility, mirroring how brokers price their own books. Both numbers live in `data/markets.ts` and become per-asset admin settings in the OTC engine task.
- **Practice starting balance** moved to `trading.practiceStartBalance` (default $10,000) and the refill threshold to `trading.practiceRefillBelow` (0 = always allowed), matching Quotex's refillable demo account.
